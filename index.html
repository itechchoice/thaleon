Note
This build runs on Apple Silicon Macs only (M1, M2, M3, M4…). Intel-based Macs are not supported yet. Not sure which you have? Click the Apple menu → About This Mac and look for “Apple M…” under Chip.

What it does

Fund research & market desk

Look up any fund's NAV, fees, and KPIs, or ask about SC / BNM / Bursa news and FX — answered with cited, dated sources.

Client management

Search clients, create or edit profiles from a KYC document, and record or adjust their holdings — in plain language.

Portfolios & X-Ray

Build investment proposals, X-Ray a client's portfolio for risk and exposure, and review retirement plans with what-ifs.

Pitch packs

Generate a compliant, client-ready recommendation — screened against your firm's model, guard rails, and advisor controls — and export to PDF.

Installing

  1. Download thaleon-agent.dmg using the button above.
  2. Open the .dmg and drag thaleon-agent into your Applications folder.
  3. Launch it from Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally — no security warnings.
